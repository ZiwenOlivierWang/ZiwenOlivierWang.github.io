Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of mathematical optimization, machine learning, and statistics, particularly in decision-focused learning and stochastic optimization, with applications in healthcare operations and decision-making.

My previous research experience includes work on AI for IT operations, anomaly detection, and locally deployable AI systems, areas that I continue to be interested in.
