About

Hi! I am a master's student in the Department of Statistics and Operations Research at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill , where I am pursuing an M.S. degree in Statistics, Analytics, and Data Science.

Prior to joining UNC, I served as a Research Assistant at Tsinghua University , working on AI for IT Operations (AIOps) under the supervision of Prof. Yong Zhang. I hold a B.S. degree in Actuarial Science from the Central University of Finance and Economics .

My current research interests focus on decision-focused learning and stochastic optimization at the intersection of mathematical optimization, statistics, and machine learning, with applications in healthcare operations.
